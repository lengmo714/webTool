What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is neutral placeholder copy used to preview typography and page structure before final content is ready. It helps designers evaluate spacing without distracting readers with meaningful prose.

How to generate placeholder text

  1. Choose the number of paragraphs, sentences, or words.
  2. Generate the text or let it update when settings change.
  3. Click Copy text and paste it into your layout.
