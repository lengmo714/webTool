What is a word frequency counter?

A word frequency counter identifies how often each word appears in a document. It helps writers notice repetition and lets marketers review keyword density before publishing.

How to check word frequency

  1. Paste or type your English text into the editor.
  2. Review the live list of words, counts, and density percentages.
  3. Choose whether to display the top 10, 25, 50, or 100 words.

Common uses

  • Find overused words in articles and essays.
  • Review SEO keyword density.
  • Analyze vocabulary in reports or transcripts.
