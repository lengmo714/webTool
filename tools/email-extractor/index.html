Email Extractor

Find every email address inside pasted text and turn the results into a clean, deduplicated list.

Extract email addresses from any text

This free email extractor scans pasted text for valid-looking email addresses, converts duplicates to a single entry, and produces one address per line. Processing happens locally in your browser.

How to use the email extractor

  1. Paste text containing email addresses into the first editor.
  2. Review the automatically extracted and deduplicated list.
  3. Click Copy emails to use the clean list elsewhere.

Common uses

  • Extract contacts from exported documents or logs.
  • Clean a mixed email and keyword list.
  • Deduplicate addresses before importing data.
