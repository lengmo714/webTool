    How does this password generator work?

    The generator uses your browser's cryptographic random-number source to select characters locally. Your password is not sent to this site, stored, or logged.

    How to create a strong password

    1. Choose a length of at least 16 characters when a service allows it.
    2. Keep a mix of character types enabled for an unpredictable result.
    3. Generate, copy, and save the password in a password manager.

    What makes a password stronger?

    Length is the biggest factor: longer, unique passwords are harder to guess. Avoid recycling passwords between accounts, predictable substitutions, names, dates, and words that are easy to associate with you.
