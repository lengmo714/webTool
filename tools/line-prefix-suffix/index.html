Line Prefix & Suffix

Wrap every line with the same opening and closing text for quick list formatting.

Advertisement

How to wrap lines

  1. Paste a list with one entry per line.
  2. Enter a prefix, a suffix, or both.
  3. Choose how to treat blank lines, then copy the result.

Useful line formatting examples

Add a bullet and space before each item, put quotation marks around labels, or surround lines with opening and closing tags. Prefixes and suffixes are literal text: backslash sequences are not expanded and content is not HTML-escaped.

Whitespace and final newlines

Existing spaces are preserved. Blank lines can remain unchanged or receive your wrapper. A final newline stays a final newline and does not create an extra wrapped record. Line endings are normalized to LF. Empty input produces empty output.

Does my input leave the browser?

Your input is processed locally with JavaScript. No upload or account is needed.
