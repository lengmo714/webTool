    What does this Roman numeral converter do?

    This tool converts modern whole numbers into standard Roman numerals and validates Roman numeral input before converting it back into an integer. It is useful for outlines, clocks, book sections, product names, and other contexts where Roman numerals still appear.

    How to convert Roman numerals online

    1. Enter either a whole number from 1 to 3999 or a Roman numeral from I to MMMCMXCIX.
    2. Select the direction you need: Number to Roman or Roman to Number.
    3. Copy the result once the conversion and validation finish.

    Why strict validation matters

    • It catches non-standard inputs such as IL or IC that look plausible but are not valid Roman numerals.
    • It normalizes lowercase and spaced input into clean uppercase output.
    • It keeps the result in the common modern range used by most Roman numeral charts and style guides.
